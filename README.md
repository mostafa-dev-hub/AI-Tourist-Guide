# AI Tourist Guide – Saudi Arabia 2034

## Overview
The **Smart World Cup Companion App** is an AI-powered mobile application designed to enhance the experience of international visitors during the **2034 FIFA World Cup in Saudi Arabia**.  
This all-in-one platform brings together essential features to help fans stay informed, connected, and safe — while also enjoying the rich culture and hospitality of Saudi Arabia.  

> **Note:** The app might still contains some errors and incomplete features.

---

## Key Features

- **Stadium & Match Info**  
  Access detailed information on all World Cup stadiums, match schedules, and teams.

- **Dynamic Tickit Pricing**  
  Applying famous Machine Learning Algorithms for dynamic ticket pricing based on various features.

- **Ticket Booking Integration**  
  Seamless ticket purchase and management through official providers.

- **Personalized Recommendations**  
  Using AI, the app suggests restaurants, hotels, attractions, and cultural experiences based on user preferences and current location.

- **AI Chatbot Support**  
  Get instant answers to common questions about matches, travel, and local services via an intelligent assistant.

- **Built-in Translator**  
  Communicate with ease across languages through an integrated translation tool tailored for real-time conversations.

- **Virtual Reality Navigation**  
  Use VR to explore stadiums, cities, and attractions - supported with Computer Vision Capabilities -.

- **Traffic & Transportation Insights**  
  Receive up-to-date traffic conditions and the best transportation options to reach your destination without delay.

- **Live Match Updates & Fan Communities**  
  Stay connected with instant scores, highlights, and join live AI-driven fan discussions to share the thrill.

- **Safety & Alerts**  
  Get emergency alerts regarding weather status.

- **Behavioral AI Enhancements**  
  AI learns from user interactions to offer better, smarter suggestions over time.
